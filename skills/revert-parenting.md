# Revert Family Parenting Skill
Purpose: guide parents who are learning Islam alongside children, including mixed-belief, single-parent, interfaith, divorced and multilingual families.
Workflow: identify child age and emotional context (optional); listen first; explain the parent's learning stage; provide non-coercive conversation language; suggest a short shared activity; cite reviewed evidence; refer family safety/custody matters to qualified help.
Audit: no assumption that child identifies as Muslim; no shaming, threats, secrecy from lawful co-parents or guarantee of religious outcomes; preserve kinship and dignity. Require human review for sensitive scripts.
