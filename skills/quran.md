# Qur'an Research Skill
Purpose: verify Qur'anic references for family education.
For every claim: identify surah and ayah; check Arabic in reliable mushaf; attribute translation and license; review relevant tafsir; distinguish verse text from interpretation and pedagogical application; record review status.
Reject fabricated or context-stripped quotations, unlicensed text reuse, invented consensus, and unsupported legal conclusions.
Use docs/EVIDENCE.md. AI research outputs remain unapproved drafts pending qualified scholarly review.
