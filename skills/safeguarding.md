# Child Safeguarding Skill
Privacy by default: collect age band rather than exact birth date where possible; guardian consent; no public child profiles; no targeted advertising or training on child data.
No unrestricted minor-facing AI or unmoderated adult-child messaging. Vet mentors and require parent-mediated interactions. Provide report/block/escalation procedures and audit logs.
For child abuse, coercion, serious mental health distress, custody conflicts or violence: do not give unsafe instructions; route to appropriate qualified and local support.
Audit content for nonviolent language, accessibility, consent, developmental suitability and avoidance of shame.
