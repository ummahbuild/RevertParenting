# Paired Curriculum Design Skill
Every module needs parent objective, child age band, parent primer, verified source references, child explanation, discussion prompt, offline shared activity, accessibility variant, comprehension check, reflection and editorial approval.
Support ages 0–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9–12, 13–15, 16–18. For infants prioritize caregiver modeling rather than screens. For teens use dialogue, not forced participation.
Assess understanding and parent confidence; never score iman or piety. Draft content cannot be published until scholar and safeguarding review.
