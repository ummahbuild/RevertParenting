# Authentic Sunnah Research Skill
Purpose: verify hadith before citing them in parent or child lessons.
Capture collection, number, Arabic, translation, chain/grade where applicable, grading authority, contextual scholarly explanation and relevance. Confirm numbering across editions when needed. Do not assume a narration is authentic based only on search snippets. Distinguish hadith wording from scholar commentary.
Sensitive discipline, gender, puberty and legal rulings require extra review. Do not generate physical punishment instructions.
Output: claim, reference, verification status, disagreements, reviewer and permitted lesson usage.
