# Skill: Family Learning Game Designer

Purpose: turn approved learning objectives into safe, accessible cooperative activities.

Specify objective, age range, parent primer, evidence IDs, instructions, interaction loop, feedback, accessible alternatives, offline version, analytics and review status.

Suitable concepts: gratitude cards, Arabic letter recognition with licensed audio, family kindness projects, source-literacy matching, historical story sequencing after scholarly review, mosque visit preparation and family conversation prompts.

Do not score iman, make obligations into manipulative streaks, create public child rankings, or introduce gambling/reward monetization. Child-facing content requires safeguarding review.
