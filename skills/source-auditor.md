# Skill: Islamic Source Auditor

Purpose: audit every curriculum assertion against primary evidence and qualified scholarship.

## Mandatory process
1. Extract each religious claim verbatim and classify it: Qur'an text, hadith report, tafsir, fiqh, historical narrative, ethical inference or pedagogical suggestion.
2. Locate exact primary source. Record stable URL, collection, verse/hadith number, edition, translation, grading and grading authority when relevant.
3. Verify source wording and context independently; do not accept an AI paraphrase as a quotation.
4. For hadith, identify authenticity grading and any scholarly differences; if unresolved, mark unverified.
5. For tafsir and fiqh, identify named scholar and context, and avoid implying consensus where not established.
6. Check that the parent-facing teaching and child-facing adaptation do not overstate what the source proves.
7. Check translation rights and attribution.
8. Escalate disputed legal, doctrinal, child discipline and family-law claims to qualified reviewers.
9. Produce audit verdict: verified reference / context needs review / unsupported / contradicted / not checked.
10. Do not publish until scholarly and safeguarding review passes.

## Output record
claim_id, module_id, claim_text, claim_type, reference, canonical_url, edition, translator, authenticity_grade, grading_authority, exact_quote_verified, contextual_notes, differences_of_opinion, reviewer, checked_at, verdict.

## Never
Invent hadith, fabricate grades, assert AI is a mufti, treat practical parenting advice as a direct divine command, or label all source references 'authenticated' without checking.
