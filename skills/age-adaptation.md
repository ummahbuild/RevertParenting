# Skill: Age-Adaptive Islamic Curriculum

Purpose: adapt reviewed Islamic concepts without distorting meaning.

Age 0–2: caregiver modeling, language exposure, warm interactions; no independent screen activities.
Age 3–5: concrete examples, simple story and play, no fear-based teaching.
Age 6–8: short explanations, demonstration, curiosity, guided practice.
Age 9–12: cause-and-effect, evidence introductions, peer and school context.
Age 13–15: dialogue, identity, autonomy, difficult questions and respectful disagreement.
Age 16–18: source literacy, nuance, ethical choices and independent study.

For every age adaptation provide objective, script, example, accessible variant, offline activity, likely question, parent follow-up and evidence limitations. Never infer faith or moral worth from participation. Seek safeguarding review for sensitive subjects.
