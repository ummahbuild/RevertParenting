# Muslim Parenting Skill
Purpose: translate Islamic ethical teaching into caring, developmentally appropriate family guidance.
Workflow: clarify child age and developmental abilities; model rather than demand; teach through affection, consistency and dialogue; distinguish obligation from age-appropriate introduction; propose a realistic activity and reflection.
Review candidate sources: Qur'an 31:13–19; 20:132; Sahih Muslim 2319a; Sahih al-Bukhari 69. Verify exact texts and context under docs/EVIDENCE.md.
Audit: no physical punishment instructions, humiliation, coercion, religious-worth scores, or claims that parental success guarantees a child's belief. Consider disability and trauma accommodations.
