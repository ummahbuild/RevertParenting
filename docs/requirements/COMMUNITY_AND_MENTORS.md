# Community and Mentor System Requirements

## Parent-only community
Opt-in circles by family stage, region and language; private-by-default participation; no child profiles in community. Moderated questions and peer support.

## Mentor onboarding
Identity verification, qualifications and references where relevant, safeguarding training, disclosure of scholarly credentials and boundaries, moderation agreement, revocation and appeal.

## Mentor matching
Language, location (coarse and optional), learning goals, time zone, family situation and accessibility. No sensitive profiling or automatic judgments about religious status.

## Communication
Parent-mediated scheduling, consent and reporting, no direct adult-child messaging, clear emergency limitations, no sharing of private household data by default.

## Mosque partner dashboard
Partner profile, family-friendly facilities, revert classes, verified contact, session availability, referral links, aggregate non-sensitive reporting.

## Safety operations
Report, block, mute, moderation queues, incident escalation, appeal, evidence retention policy, reviewer audit, and emergency suspension.

## Business model
Core religious fundamentals remain accessible. Optional paid family conveniences or institutional support cannot gate safety guidance or essential source access.
