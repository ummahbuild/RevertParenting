# Parenting Scenarios and Guidance Requirements

## Standard response schema
Scenario ID, user intent, parent feelings, child's likely perspective, age bands, family composition, parent knowledge prerequisite, verified sources, interpretation caveats, listen-first script, what not to say, shared activity, referral, review status.

## Safety and epistemic rules
No coercion, threats, humiliation, physical punishment, invented religious certainty or custody evasion. Distinguish Islamic source, interpretation, parenting evidence and practical advice. High-risk subjects require qualified human review and appropriate referral.
