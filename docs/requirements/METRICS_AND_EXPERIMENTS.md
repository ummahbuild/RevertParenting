# Analytics, Research, and Product Experiments

## North-star
Parent-reported ability to guide age-appropriate Islamic learning and maintain a positive family learning relationship.

## Funnel
Landing view -> anonymous preview -> plan generated -> parent primer opened -> child explanation opened -> shared activity completed -> optional reflection -> week-four return.

## Events
preview_started, goal_selected, age_band_selected, plan_generated, primer_opened, family_activity_completed, reflection_saved, evidence_opened, content_feedback_sent, mentor_referral_requested, account_deleted.

## Privacy
No logging child names, free-text sensitive questions, conversion dates, or religious beliefs in third-party analytics. Aggregate events and honor consent.

## Experiments
Five-minute vs ten-minute default; parent-first vs family-first navigation; text vs audio primer; printable vs in-app activity; anonymous vs registration-first; mosque partner referrals vs creator outreach. Never experiment on coercion, safety or accuracy.

## Qualitative research
Interview revert parents across child ages, family structures, countries, languages and disability needs. Consent-based recruitment, voluntary stories, anonymized insights, no child interviews without proper safeguards.

## Operational metrics
Content review latency, citation accuracy, source corrections, accessibility defects, RLS test coverage, data deletion SLA, incident response time.
