# Product Mode Specifications

## Parent Mode
Home: confidence-based recommended primer; topic library; verified sources; short audio; parent journal; saved questions; teaching scripts; scholar referrals. Parent can change knowledge level per topic and hide sensitive history.

## Child Mode
Guardian-selected age band; curated lesson cards; short approved audio; story illustrations; accessible games; no unrestricted AI, public profile, messaging, religious-worth score or personalized ads. Young children use caregiver-mediated activities.

## Family Mode
Today's plan, shared lesson, offline activity timer, conversation prompts, weekly rhythm, multiple child adaptations, household reflection, flexible schedule. No forced participation or public ranking.

## Parenting Mode
Scenario search, listen-first coaching, child-development context, Qur'an/Sunnah source links, interpretation caveats, example scripts, what to avoid, qualified referrals. High-risk subjects flagged for human support.

## Qur'an Mode
Verified Arabic text, named translations, audio with rights, recitation playback controls, tafsir context, pronunciation aids, child-safe reflection. No unsupported tajwid diagnosis or invented interpretations.

## Sunnah Mode
Hadith collection and number, grade and grading authority, context, scholarly interpretation, practical family application and editorial status. Disputed reports clearly labeled.

## Community Mode
Parent-only moderated circles, vetted mentors, mosque directory, referral requests, safety reporting, opt-in notifications, no direct mentor-child contact.

## Reflection Mode
Private parent journal, gratitude, family discussion notes, optional learning milestones, export/delete and a no-shame restart pathway.

## Admin/Scholar Mode
Evidence queue, lesson diff, translator licensing, scholar and child-safety review, audit log, publish/rollback, incident moderation and versioned approvals.

## Cross-mode requirements
Clear role boundary; deep links; offline and loading states; accessibility; RTL; consent; no child-sensitive analytics; reviewed content only in public mode.
