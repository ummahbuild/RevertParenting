# Family Planner and Daily Practice Requirements

## User stories
As a busy parent, I can choose a five-minute activity and still make progress. As a guardian with children of different ages, I can see separate adaptations. As a parent in an interfaith home, I can choose respectful family activities without pressuring others.

## Planner objects
Household, guardian, age-band profile, routine template, planned activity, lesson reference, recurrence, time window, reminder consent, completion, private reflection.

## Today screen
Greeting; next recommended parent primer; child adaptation switcher; shared activity; optional schedule; recent questions; no shame-based streak.

## Week view
Editable calendar, drag/reorder, optional recurring routines, travel mode, pause week, swap activity, print schedule, accessibility preference.

## Notifications
Opt-in, device privacy mode, quiet hours, no revealing conversion status on shared lock screens. Reminders cannot imply guilt for missing prayer or lessons.

## Multiple children
One family theme, distinct explanations per age, optional child participation, no public ranking or comparative faith scores.

## Offline
Download approved activity packs, view upcoming plans, queue non-sensitive completion events, reconcile conflicts on reconnect.

## Acceptance criteria
Plan generation within a few seconds on supported devices, skip without penalty, guardian permissions, deletion/export, accessible keyboard and screen reader interactions, no sensitive third-party analytics.
