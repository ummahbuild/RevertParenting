# Localization and Cultural Adaptation

## Initial languages to assess
English, Arabic, Swahili, Urdu, French, Indonesian, Turkish and Hausa. Prioritize by parent research and qualified translator availability.

## Translation requirements
Maintain Arabic original separately from named translations; provenance and licensing; glossary for salah, dua, tawhid, iman, adab, fiqh; transliteration optional and not substitute for verified audio.

## Cultural sensitivity
Different family structures, school systems, holiday practices, mosque access, minority/majority contexts, conversion stigma and interfaith relatives. Avoid assuming one culture defines Islam.

## UI
Full RTL support, mixed Arabic/Latin text, scalable typography, local date/time, locale-aware reminders, accessible fonts, offline content packs.

## Editorial
Every translated lesson requires review of meaning, age appropriateness, religious accuracy, cultural context, reading level and accessibility. Track locale-specific approvals.
