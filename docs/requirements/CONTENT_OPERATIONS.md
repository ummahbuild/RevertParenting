# Marketing and Curriculum Content Operations

## Pipeline
Idea -> audience persona -> research -> draft -> factual/source check -> religious review where relevant -> safeguarding review -> accessibility -> localization -> approved -> scheduled -> published -> analytics -> revision.

## Content metadata
content_id, channel, persona, topic, objective, format, hook, body, CTA, sources, licensing, image brief, reviewer, review_status, scheduled_at, published_url, performance and next_revision.

## Publishing gates
Religious claims must be source-checked; no fake testimonials, invented user numbers or unverified outcomes. No identifiable child content without documented guardian consent and additional child safeguards. Distinguish product-in-development from available features.

## Repurposing
One approved parenting question becomes: SEO article, carousel, short-form video, email, community discussion prompt, printable activity and app FAQ. Preserve approved meaning and evidence across channels.

## Growth metrics
Opt-in subscribers, article engagement, qualified contributor applications, parent research participants, family lesson preview completion, mosque partnerships and privacy-safe referrals.

## Governance
Named editor and reviewer, correction process, rights management, scheduled source re-review, public corrections when necessary, unsubscribe and consent handling.
