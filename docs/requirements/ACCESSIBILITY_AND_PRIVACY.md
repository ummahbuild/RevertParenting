# Accessibility, Child Safety, and Privacy Requirements

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; screen readers; high contrast; 44px minimum targets; adjustable type; dyslexia-friendly typography options; RTL Arabic; subtitles/transcripts; audio speed; reduced motion; keyboard navigation; accessible printables; sensory-friendly modes; low-bandwidth fallback.

## Child data
No direct child accounts in MVP. Prefer age band over birth date and pseudonyms over names. No public child profiles, targeted advertising, public leaderboards, or model training using child data. No unrestricted generative AI with minors.

## Family safety
Guardian consent and scoped roles. Avoid sensitive notifications on shared devices. Co-parent and household split flows must prevent unauthorized continued access. Mentor communications remain parent-mediated.

## Security
Supabase RLS, explicit authorization tests, rate limiting, data encryption, secrets management, export/delete workflows, retention limits, audit logging, incident response and abuse reporting. Review applicable children's privacy laws before launch.

## Safeguarding escalation
Self-harm, abuse, domestic violence, custody disputes, dangerous discipline and mental health emergencies must be routed to appropriate qualified support. Never imply the app can resolve these situations on its own.

## Offline
Offline-approved content only, encrypted local storage for sensitive notes if persisted, revocation behavior, sync conflict handling, clear offline indicators.
