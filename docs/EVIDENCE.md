# Qur'an and Authentic Sunnah Audit Standard

This is a candidate source inventory, **not** scholarly approval.

## Source verification rules
- Qur'an: exact surah/ayah, Arabic text, translator name, translation license, tafsir context.
- Hadith: exact collection/number, wording, authenticity grading, grading authority, scholarly context; do not presume every narration is sahih.
- Separate revelation, translation, interpretation, empirical parenting research and practical suggestion.
- Label scholarly disagreements and never invent consensus.
- No automated fatwas or claims that AI is a scholar.
- No physical discipline instructions, threats, shame or coercive conversion tactics.
- Every child-facing lesson requires safeguarding and qualified religious review.

## Audit workflow
Draft -> verify text/reference -> authenticate hadith -> review interpretation and fiqh -> check child safety and developmental appropriateness -> verify licenses -> reviewer approval -> publish with version -> periodic re-review.

## Answer template
1. Empathetic parent guidance.
2. Child-friendly words.
3. Verifiable sources and what they do/don't establish.
4. Difference of opinion / uncertainty.
5. Offline practice and qualified human referral when needed.
