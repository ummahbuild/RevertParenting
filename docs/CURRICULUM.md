# RevertParenting Curriculum — Draft Specification

All lesson content is pending qualified scholar and child-safeguarding review.

## Learning model
Parent primer -> child explanation -> shared practice -> parent reflection. Differentiate age bands 0–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9–12, 13–15 and 16–18. Offer five-, ten- and twenty-minute modes.

## First month themes
Week 1: parent's journey, listening, foundations of belief, gratitude.
Week 2: Prophet's example of mercy, introduction to Qur'an and Al-Fatihah.
Week 3: wudu, salah introduction, everyday dua.
Week 4: character, family ties, Muslim community, sustainable routines.

## Lesson 001: Gratitude
Parent objective: understand gratitude without denying hardship.
Evidence candidate: Qur'an 14:7 (verify context and translation).
Child ages 3–5: identify three things they appreciate and learn Alhamdulillah.
Ages 6–8: make a thank-you card.
Ages 9–12: identify an act of gratitude.
Teens: discuss gratitude and difficult feelings together.
Shared practice: each family member shares one appreciation and one kind act.
Reflection: 'What did my child ask?' No religious-worth score.

## Lesson 002: Why I became Muslim
Parent objective: explain personal journey honestly, with reassurance.
Child explanation: 'I've been learning about Islam and chose to become Muslim. You can ask me questions and we can learn together.'
Activity: draw or write questions for a family discussion.
Safety: never pressure a child to claim a belief; respect co-parenting agreements.

## Editorial requirements
Every lesson includes source manifest, translation rights, named reviewers, safeguarding checks, localization status, accessibility variant and version history. Only approved content reaches production.
