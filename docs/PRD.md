# RevertParenting — Product Requirements

## Problem
A parent may still be learning salah, Qur'an and Islamic foundations while children ask questions about faith, identity, holidays, grandparents and school. Existing products often split adult revert instruction from children's learning.

## Positioning
**Learn it as a parent. Explain it to your child. Live it together.**

## Personas
New revert with toddler, school-age child or teenager; single parent; interfaith/co-parenting household; parent with non-Muslim relatives; returning Muslim; multilingual or disabled family; child who knows more than parent.

## P0 functional requirements
1. Anonymous preview and optional account registration.
2. Sensitive-question-optional onboarding: parent confidence, child age band, languages, available minutes, goals, family context.
3. Parent and child progress tracked separately; child can be ahead of parent.
4. Explainable 7/30-day personalized pathways.
5. Each lesson has parent primer, verified evidence, age-adapted child explanation, offline family activity, reflection.
6. Parent academy for tawhid, pillars, Qur'an, salah, Seerah, akhlaq and parenting.
7. Family daily plan with 5/10/20-minute options and no punitive streaks.
8. Reviewed scenario guidance: explaining conversion, prayer reluctance, teens' doubts, school, holidays, relatives, interfaith and co-parenting.
9. Source-grounded parent-only AI; no unrestricted child AI chat.
10. Editorial publishing gates for scholarly, safeguarding and licensing review.
11. Privacy-first guardian roles, child data minimization, export and deletion.
12. Captions, RTL, screen reader, offline activity packs and sensory-friendly modes.

## P1
Vetted parent mentors, mosque referrals, school accommodation letters, family circles, multilingual narration, printable workbooks, Ramadan and Eid planners.

## Key screens
Welcome, Explore, Onboarding, Household, Pathway, Today, Parent Primer, Child Lesson, Learn Together, Activity, Ask, Evidence Detail, Family Plan, Reflection, Community, Privacy, Editorial Review.

Every screen: loading, empty, error, offline, accessible and permission-denied states.

## Safety
No child religious-worth scoring; no coercive conversion tactics; no physical punishment instructions; no advice to violate custody arrangements. Distinguish fiqh disagreements; refer qualified scholars for rulings. Child information never used for targeted ads or model training.

## Success metrics
Parent-reported confidence, first shared lesson completion, offline activity adoption, week-four family retention, citation accuracy and review turnaround. Targets require user research validation.

## Definition of done
Acceptance criteria, accessibility, responsive UI, tests, privacy and safeguarding checks, source provenance, review approval, analytics, rollback plan.
