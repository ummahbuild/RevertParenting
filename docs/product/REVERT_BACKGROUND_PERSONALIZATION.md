# Revert Background Personalization — Requirements

## Principle
Personalize to self-reported learning needs and family context, not stereotypes or inferred religious identity. Background questions are optional, editable, deletable, and should not gate learning.

## Profile dimensions
- Revert journey stage: exploring, recent, established, returning to practice, prefer not to say.
- Prior religious context: Christian, Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist, nonreligious, other, prefer not to say. Never assume doctrine or family conflict from this field.
- Islamic learning confidence by topic: shahadah, tawhid, salah, Qur'an, Arabic, Seerah, parenting, fiqh.
- Parent's primary learning language, reading comfort, audio preference, transliteration preference.
- Child age bands and preferred learning languages; optional accessibility needs.
- Household context: single parent, co-parent, interfaith, extended family, multiple guardians; all optional.
- Local context: country/region or manually selected community; precise location not required.
- Family goals, available minutes, preferred learning schedule, mentor preference.
- Optional madhhab or trusted scholar preference, with no forced default or assumption of correctness.

## Onboarding steps
1. Anonymous explore.
2. Choose parent goals and current knowledge.
3. Optionally describe revert background and family circumstances.
4. Select adult learning language and child learning language independently.
5. Add child age bands, not mandatory legal names/birth dates.
6. Choose accessibility and offline needs.
7. Preview explainable learning plan.
8. Optional account creation and explicit consent to store profile.

## Recommendation contract
Return topic, reason, age variant, language, source approval, estimated minutes, offline alternative. Example reason: 'Suggested because you chose learning salah and a 5-minute session.' Never: 'Because former Christians believe X.'

## Privacy
Prior religion, conversion history and family beliefs are sensitive. Store only with explicit informed consent; do not send to marketing analytics, ad platforms or model training. Allow deletion of a single background field without deleting account.

## Anti-profiling tests
Different religious backgrounds with identical goals and knowledge must receive equivalent foundational lessons unless a user explicitly requests context-specific guidance. Never infer child's faith from attendance, behavior or engagement.
