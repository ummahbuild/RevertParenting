# RevertParenting

**Learn Islam. Guide your children. Grow together.**

An ummah.build initiative supporting parents who embraced Islam, parents returning to practice, and families learning the Deen together.

## Why
Revert courses often teach adults. Islamic kids' apps often teach children. RevertParenting bridges them with a **dual-learning engine**: parent primer → child-friendly explanation → shared offline practice → reflection.

## Product pillars
- Parent Academy: Islam, Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, parenting confidence
- Learn Together: age-adaptive paired lessons and activities
- Family Life: prayer, school, relatives, holidays, co-parenting
- Ask with Evidence: reviewed sources, uncertainty, scholar referral
- Community: vetted mentors and family circles
- Curriculum Studio: review, translation, accessibility, versioning
