# Ethical Growth Engine

## Funnel
Awareness -> free 30-day starter guide -> opt-in email -> first parent primer -> first family activity -> parent feedback -> referral to trusted family/mosque.

## Audience campaigns
1. Newly reverted parent with young children: 'What do I teach first?'
2. Revert with teenager: 'How do I talk about my faith without pushing my child away?'
3. Single parent: 'Five minutes of learning together.'
4. Interfaith household: 'Build understanding across family differences.'
5. Parent returning to Islam: 'Restart with your family, without shame.'
6. Mosque coordinator: 'Give new Muslim parents a structured family pathway.'

## Content operations
Content idea -> source research -> parenting and religious review -> copy and visual brief -> localization -> scheduled publishing -> feedback -> source re-review. Store owner, channel, CTA, evidence, status, consent and results.

## Weekly cadence hypothesis
Monday parent question; Tuesday micro-lesson; Wednesday family activity; Thursday builder update; Friday Qur'an/Sunnah context; Saturday parent story (consent-based); Sunday family reflection.

## SEO clusters
how to teach Islam to children as a new Muslim; revert parent Islamic parenting; explaining conversion to children; learning salah with kids; Muslim parenting in interfaith families; teaching Qur'an when you can't read Arabic; Muslim family Ramadan starter plan.

## Analytics
Consent-based, aggregate metrics: landing-to-guide conversion, first lesson completion, parent confidence pre/post, activity reuse, retention and partner referrals. No sensitive child behavior advertising profiles.

## Experiments
Test empathetic hero headlines, five-minute lesson preview, downloadable starter pack and mosque partner referrals. Avoid guilt/fear messaging and unverifiable efficacy claims.
