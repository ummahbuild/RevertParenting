# RevertParenting — Creative Production System

## Brand
Forest #174C43; sage #D8E9D5; cream #FAF6ED; gold #D9AA67; ink #273D36. Friendly typography, quiet layouts, inclusive family scenes, no manipulative faith gamification.

## Reusable formats
- 9:16 Reels/TikTok: hook 0–3 seconds, one real family situation, one helpful insight, one CTA.
- 4:5 Instagram carousel: 6–8 panels, high contrast, short sentences, alt text.
- 1:1 community graphic: one key statement, subtitle, project link.
- LinkedIn article: founder insight, unmet need, product differentiator, call for expertise.
- Mosque newsletter: parent problem, resource, curriculum review status, partnership CTA.
- Email: subject, preview, 150–300 word body, one CTA and unsubscribe.

## Content brief fields
Campaign ID, target persona, parent pain point, message, full script, storyboard, references, source review, child safeguarding, illustration rights, channel, dimensions, CTA, owner, schedule, approval, performance.

## Repurposing workflow
One approved article -> 6-slide carousel -> 30-second video -> short email -> printable conversation card -> app FAQ. Maintain provenance and content version across formats.

## Editorial checklist
Correct feature availability; verified religious claims; child privacy and consent; accessible captions and alt text; clear CTA; no fabricated outcomes; source and license records.
