# RevertParenting Partner and Recruitment Campaign

## Priority partner personas
Mosque revert coordinators, qualified scholars, Muslim parenting educators, revert mothers/fathers, Islamic school counselors, child-development specialists, safeguarding reviewers, translators, illustrators, Expo/Next.js engineers.

## Outreach sequence
Day 0: personalized invitation explaining the missing parent-child learning bridge.
Day 4: send a short sample parent primer and family activity (reviewed before external release).
Day 10: invite to a 20-minute discovery interview or curriculum review.
Day 21: optional beta partnership follow-up. Respect opt-outs and do not automate unsolicited mass outreach.

## Interview prompts
1. What did your children ask when you embraced Islam?
2. Which question was hardest to answer?
3. What learning materials were missing?
4. Which relatives or school situations were most complicated?
5. What would make a five-minute lesson useful?
6. What support would you trust from a mosque or mentor?
7. What should the app never ask or store?
8. How do you want a child to participate without pressure?
9. What accessibility or language needs exist?
10. Would you review an early prototype?

## Research safeguards
Consent before recording; no children's identifying information; voluntary participation; no inference of faith from app activity; secure handling of sensitive stories.

## Distribution assets
Founder story, one-page mission, demo link (once deployed), parent research form, mosque partner deck, educator advisory invitation, GitHub contributor issue templates.
