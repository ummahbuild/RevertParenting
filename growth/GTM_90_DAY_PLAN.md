# RevertParenting — 90-Day Go-to-Market Plan

Status: proposed; no campaigns are currently claimed as launched.

## Positioning
Learn Islam as a parent. Explain it to your child. Practice together as a family.

## Segments
New revert parents with toddlers; parents with school-age children; parents with teenagers; single parents; interfaith households; parents returning to practice; mosque revert coordinators; qualified scholars and educators.

## Days 1–15: Research and positioning
Interview 20–30 opt-in parents across ages and household situations; record anonymized unmet needs. Audit competing revert and children's learning tools. Test three hero headlines and a sample family lesson. Recruit a qualified scholar and safeguarding advisory group.

## Days 16–30: Foundational content
Publish approved non-sensitive product explainers, builder recruitment, 30-day starter guide landing page, waitlist with consent and unsubscribe, partner FAQ, and early demo video. Do not publish religious curriculum until reviewed.

## Days 31–60: Audience building
Weekly series: Questions Kids Ask, Learning Together, Five-Minute Family, Behind the Build, Scholar Review Explained. Repurpose approved ideas into TikTok/Reels, Instagram carousel, LinkedIn, email and SEO. Outreach to mosque revert programs, parenting educators and community organizers with personalized opt-in invitations.

## Days 61–90: Closed beta and partnerships
Recruit 30–50 volunteer parent testers with informed consent. Evaluate first family activity, clarity of explanations, confidence, privacy and accessibility. Pilot with 2–3 willing community partners. Publish corrections transparently and prioritize retention over vanity metrics.

## Weekly publishing rhythm
Monday: parent question; Tuesday: paired lesson preview; Wednesday: family activity; Thursday: builder update; Friday: reviewed source-literacy explainer; Saturday: consent-based parent interview; Sunday: family reflection.

## Growth funnel
Content -> helpful free preview -> optional email -> first family plan -> first activity -> feedback -> referral. Avoid manipulative streaks, conversion pressure or advertising to children.

## KPIs to validate
Visitor-to-preview, preview-to-opt-in, first activity completion, parent confidence before/after, week-four return, qualified reviewer recruitment, mosque partner referrals, unsubscribes, content correction rate.

## Production roles
Founder/editor, parent researcher, qualified religious reviewer, child-development/safeguarding reviewer, growth lead, designer, engineer, translation editor. No invented endorsements or fabricated testimonials.
