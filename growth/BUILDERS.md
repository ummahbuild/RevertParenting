# Call for Builders — RevertParenting

## Full announcement
**We're building RevertParenting, and we're looking for builders.**

Imagine embracing Islam while already raising children. You're learning how to pray, read Qur'an and understand your faith. Meanwhile your child asks: Why did you become Muslim? Do we have to pray? What about our grandparents and holidays?

There are apps for adults learning Islam. There are apps for Muslim children. **Who helps the parent learning Islam while guiding an entire family?**

RevertParenting is a family-centered learning companion from ummah.build. It pairs authentic Islamic knowledge with age-sensitive teaching guidance and practical activities parents and children can experience together.

We're looking for revert parents, Muslim parents, qualified scholars, educators, child-development and safeguarding professionals, designers, illustrators, translators, developers, researchers, mosque leaders and community organizers.

We especially want lived experience from parents who embraced Islam while raising children. Your perspective should shape the product, not just validate it after launch.

Contribute: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting

## Contributor intake
Ask: role, relevant experience, languages, availability, areas of interest, permission to contact. Avoid collecting personal conversion histories unless voluntarily shared with informed consent.

## Outreach targets
Revert support groups, mosque convert coordinators, Muslim parent educators, Islamic studies teachers, Muslim family therapists, university Muslim societies, accessibility specialists, open-source developer communities.

## Contribution standards
Religious material needs qualified human approval; do not present drafts as fatwas. Child stories need explicit consent and privacy safeguards. No public child profiles, conversion pressure or faith ranking.
