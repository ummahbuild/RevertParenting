# RevertParenting Dispatch — Issue 01

Subject: Building the missing bridge for Muslim families
Preheader: Why we're creating RevertParenting, what we're building first, and how you can help.

Assalamu alaikum,

A parent can embrace Islam while already raising children who have their own routines, questions and understanding of the world.

That creates a challenge many educational products don't directly address. The parent is learning how to pray, understand Qur'an and build new habits. At the same time, their children want to know what these changes mean for the family.

We're building RevertParenting to connect those experiences.

Our core learning model is simple:
**Learn as a parent. Explain to your child. Practice together.**

The first product experiences we're designing include a parent academy, child-friendly lesson adaptations, five-minute family activities, real-life parenting scenarios, and a private family planner.

We're also developing a strict religious-content review process. Qur'an references need accurate text and context. Hadith require verification and appropriate grading. Interpretations should be identified clearly. Qualified scholars and safeguarding specialists must review lessons before release.

This project is still in development. The early web and mobile prototypes are not a finished product, and the curriculum is not yet scholar-approved.

**How you can help**
If you're a revert parent, tell us what questions your children asked and what support you wished you had. If you're a scholar, teacher, designer, developer, translator or child-development specialist, we'd welcome your expertise.

Explore the repository: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting

Our goal is not to replace parents, teachers or communities. It's to give families a thoughtful place to begin learning together.
