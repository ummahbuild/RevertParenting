# RevertParenting — Complete FAQ and Objection Handling Copy

## Is RevertParenting only for new Muslims?
It is designed primarily for parents who embraced Islam and are learning alongside their children. Parents returning to practice and families with different learning levels may also find it useful.

## Do I need to know Arabic?
No. The planned experience starts with accessible explanations and offers reviewed audio, attributed translations and gradual learning. Qualified teachers remain important for recitation and detailed study.

## What if my child is not Muslim?
The product is designed to support respectful conversations and learning. It does not pressure children to identify with a belief or claim an app can determine their faith.

## What if my spouse or co-parent is not Muslim?
The planned guidance includes respectful communication, shared parenting boundaries and consideration for existing legal agreements. It is not a substitute for legal advice or qualified religious guidance.

## What if I don't know how to pray?
You can start with beginner learning material and a family activity. Prayer instruction and jurisprudential differences require verified teaching and qualified review.

## Is the content authentic?
The project has a documented Qur'an and Sunnah verification process, but the curriculum is still in draft and not yet scholar-approved. We will distinguish verified sources, interpretations and practical parenting suggestions.

## Is there an AI assistant?
A parent-facing, source-grounded guidance assistant is planned. It will not act as a mufti or offer unrestricted child-facing chat.

## Will my children be graded on faith?
No. RevertParenting will not score religious worth, rank children by piety or punish missed activities.

## How much will it cost?
Pricing has not been finalized. The intention is to keep core introductory religious learning accessible while exploring optional family and institutional services.

## Is the app available now?
RevertParenting is in development. The repository includes early web and Expo prototypes, specifications and draft content, not a production launch.

## How can I help?
Revert parents, scholars, teachers, child-development experts, safeguarding specialists, designers, developers and translators can contribute through https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting.

## How will you protect family information?
The product requirements prioritize guardian permissions, minimal child data, private reflections, deletion/export and no targeted advertising using children's information. These safeguards must be implemented and tested before launch.

## Can mosques use it?
Mosque partner pathways and educator tools are planned. We welcome feedback from revert coordinators and Islamic educators.

## What if I disagree with a lesson?
The product should provide source context, identify recognized scholarly differences and offer a correction process. Complex rulings should be referred to qualified scholars.

## What if my child has a disability or different learning needs?
The roadmap includes captions, accessible text, offline activities, sensory-friendly modes, assistive communication and adaptable lesson formats.

## Why not just use YouTube or existing Islamic apps?
Videos and existing apps can be useful. RevertParenting's proposed difference is the connected parent-child journey: the parent learns, receives age-sensitive teaching guidance and practices together with their family.

## Objection: I don't have time.
Start with a five-minute conversation or activity. The planned routine system is flexible and does not penalize missed days.

## Objection: I'm embarrassed about what I don't know.
The experience is designed without public knowledge rankings or religious-worth scores. You can begin anonymously with a preview.

## Objection: My teenager may not want to participate.
Teen pathways prioritize dialogue, privacy, independent questions and respectful participation rather than forced engagement.

## Objection: I want scholarly reliability.
That is why the project requires primary-source verification, qualified reviewers, context and approval gates. Unreviewed content is not presented as authoritative.
