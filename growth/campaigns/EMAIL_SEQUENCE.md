# RevertParenting — Complete Email Sequences

Status: ready-to-edit marketing drafts. Only send to opted-in recipients.

## Family welcome 1 — Subject: You don't need every answer to begin
Assalamu alaikum,
If you're learning Islam while raising children, you're not the only parent balancing questions and responsibilities. Your child might ask about salah, Ramadan or your personal journey before you feel ready to explain it.
We're building RevertParenting to make those moments easier to approach: first a short lesson for you, then words suited to your child's age, followed by something meaningful you can do together.
You can begin with one conversation. You don't need a perfect schedule.
RevertParenting — Learn Islam. Grow together.

## Family welcome 2 — Subject: When your child asks 'Why?'
Assalamu alaikum,
Sometimes the most helpful response is: 'That's a thoughtful question. I'm learning too. Let's explore it together.'
RevertParenting is designed around that kind of conversation. We want parents to have reliable sources, simple explanations and the confidence to listen rather than rush to an answer.
What question has your child asked that you'd like help exploring? You can share feedback voluntarily; please don't include identifying information about your child.

## Family welcome 3 — Subject: Your first five-minute family activity
Assalamu alaikum,
Try this simple activity tonight: each person shares one thing they appreciated today and one kind action they'd like to take tomorrow.
Ask your child what they noticed. Listen without correcting every answer. The goal is a moment of connection and reflection.
Our curriculum will pair activities like this with verified Islamic learning after qualified review.
Small, repeatable moments can help build a welcoming family rhythm.
