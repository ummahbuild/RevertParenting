# Community Recruitment — Copy-Ready Campaigns

## Parent research invitation
**Did you embrace Islam while raising children?**
We're building RevertParenting, a learning companion for families navigating Islam together. We'd love to hear what questions your children asked, what resources helped, and what support was missing. Participation is voluntary, and you never need to share identifying information about your children. Help shape the product: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting

## Scholar advisory invitation
**Help make Islamic family education accurate and responsible.**
RevertParenting is seeking qualified scholars and Islamic educators to review Qur'an references, hadith authenticity, scholarly interpretation and age-appropriate curriculum. We are building human approval into the publishing workflow. Explore the project: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting

## Parenting expert invitation
**Help families learn with kindness and developmental care.**
We're looking for child-development experts, Muslim parenting educators and safeguarding professionals to review activities, difficult-conversation guidance and accessibility. The goal is a supportive, evidence-aware experience that respects children's dignity and family relationships.

## Engineer recruitment
**Build a family learning experience, not another content feed.**
RevertParenting needs Expo, Next.js, Supabase, TypeScript, accessibility, curriculum tooling, offline support and privacy-focused engineering. Our central UX is simple: parent learns, child understands, family practices. Contribute: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting

## Designer recruitment
**Design a gentle, modern Islamic family experience.**
We're seeking designers and illustrators to create parent learning, child-friendly stories, shared activities, and welcoming family flows. Avoid manipulative gamification and stereotyped imagery. Help us design something that works for different ages, abilities and family structures.

## Mosque coordinator invitation
**What happens after a parent takes Shahadah?**
Adult convert classes are important, but parents may need help explaining their journey to children and building family routines. RevertParenting is being developed to support that bridge. We'd welcome your feedback on a future family starter program and mosque partnership model.
