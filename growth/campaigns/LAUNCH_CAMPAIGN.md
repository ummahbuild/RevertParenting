### Positioning explainer
There are courses for adults who recently embraced Islam. There are stories and games for Muslim children. But parenting while learning Islam introduces a different challenge: you are discovering your Deen while guiding a child with questions, feelings, friendships and an identity of their own. RevertParenting brings parent learning, child-friendly explanations and shared activities into one guided experience.

### Long-form launch announcement
**Introducing RevertParenting: a place for families learning Islam together.**

When a parent embraces Islam, their journey rarely happens alone. A child might ask why the family prays differently, what Ramadan means, or why a parent has changed their daily routine. The parent might be learning those answers at the same time.

That is the problem RevertParenting is being built to solve.

Rather than giving parents another endless library of videos, we're designing a guided family learning experience. Parents learn the foundations with verified sources. They receive developmentally appropriate explanations and conversation prompts for their children. Then the family tries a short activity together, offline or in the app.

The platform is designed for different family realities: toddlers, school-age children, teenagers, single parents, interfaith households, families with non-Muslim relatives, and parents returning to practice.

We are building with the Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, qualified scholarly review, child safeguarding, and lived experience from revert families at the center. The product is in development, and the curriculum will not be published as approved religious instruction until reviewed.

We're inviting revert parents, educators, scholars, designers, engineers, translators and community organizers to help build it.

**Learn Islam. Guide your children. Grow together.**
Contribute: https://github.com/ummahbuild/RevertParenting
