## Localization copy rules
Translate meaning rather than word-for-word slogans; avoid assumptions about local family structures, holidays and school systems. Preserve religious source attribution. Do not publish a locale until interface, privacy notices and relevant curriculum have qualified review.

## Localized content themes
East Africa: English/Swahili household switching, mosque community and school context.
Europe: multilingual families, religious identity, school accommodations and interfaith relatives.
North America: school calendars, grandparents and mixed-belief families.
South/Southeast Asia: family language variation, local cultural customs and mosque learning.
These are research hypotheses, not stereotypes or default rules.
