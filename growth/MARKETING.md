# Brand, Messaging, and Marketing Engine

## Brand promise
**Learn Islam. Guide your children. Grow together.**

## Primary headline
You're learning Islam. Your children are learning from you. Grow together.

## Supporting copy
RevertParenting helps parents who embraced Islam learn the Deen, explain it with confidence, and build meaningful family routines through verified Islamic learning, child-friendly guidance and activities done together.

## Differentiator
Revert learning apps focus on the adult. Islamic children's apps focus on the child. RevertParenting bridges both: parent primer -> age-specific explanation -> family practice -> reflection.

## Alternative headlines
- You don't have to know everything to begin learning together.
- A family learning Islam, one day at a time.
- From your first questions to your family's everyday moments.
- Not just lessons for parents or kids. A pathway for the whole family.
- Your journey to Islam can be a family journey.

## Messaging rules
Lead with empathy, agency and support. Avoid guilt, fear, religious-worth rankings, guaranteed religious outcomes or shaming parents. Do not imply all children must convert. Do not claim scholarly endorsement before it exists.

## Landing page structure
Hero + waitlist -> who it is for -> parent/child paired learning demo -> real-life scenarios -> verified Qur'an/Sunnah and review process -> curriculum preview -> community -> builder and scholar calls -> FAQ -> email signup.

## FAQ
Q: Must I already know Arabic? A: No. Begin with explanations, verified audio and gradual practice.
Q: What if my child isn't Muslim? A: The platform helps with respectful conversation and learning, not pressure.
Q: Is this a fatwa service? A: No. Religious interpretation is reviewed and complex questions are referred to qualified scholars.
Q: Is it free? A: Core beginner learning is planned to be accessible; final pricing is not set.
Q: Can I contribute? A: Yes, through GitHub and reviewed content proposals.

## Content pillars
Parent stories (consent-based); real questions children ask; learn together lesson demos; Qur'an/Sunnah evidence with context; gentle parenting skills; school/grandparent scenarios; behind-the-build; community partnerships.
