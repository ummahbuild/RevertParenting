# RevertParenting — Family Activity Library

Status: draft activities. Religious evidence, any claims about worship, and age-sensitive content require review before publication.

## Activity format
ID; learning objective; age bands; parent preparation; materials; steps; conversation prompts; accessibility alternative; duration; source review status; child-safety review status.

### ACT-001 Question Jar
Age: 6–18. Time: 10 min. Materials: paper, container (optional).
Parent: Explain that no question is embarrassing and not every answer must be immediate.
Steps: Each person writes or says one question. Choose one. Parent models 'I don't know yet; let's find a reliable source.' Save other questions privately.
Prompt: 'What made you wonder about that?'
Accessible: verbal, drawing, AAC or digital text. No public sharing.

### ACT-002 Gratitude Circle
Age: 3–18. Time: 5 min.
Parent: Make space for both gratitude and difficult feelings.
Steps: Share one appreciation; passing is allowed. Choose one act of kindness.
Prompt: 'Who helped you today?'
Accessible: pictures, gestures, speech or writing.
Evidence candidate: Qur'an 14:7; verify context before religious publication.

### ACT-003 First Mosque Visit Checklist
Age: all, caregiver-guided. Time: 15 min preparation.
Steps: Check accessibility, child facilities, prayer areas, contacts and transport. Talk about what a mosque is for. Ask the child what they hope to see. Reflect afterward.
Prompt: 'What would help you feel comfortable there?'
No promise that every mosque has identical facilities.

### ACT-004 Kindness at Home
Age: 3–18. Time: 10 min.
Steps: Each person identifies a helpful act, chooses one achievable action and reflects without comparison.
Prompt: 'How did it feel to help someone?'
No public points or moral ranking.

### ACT-005 Learning Salah Together
Age: caregiver-led. Time: 10 min.
Parent: Review verified prayer instruction and consult a qualified teacher for jurisprudential differences.
Steps: Observe a reviewed demonstration, identify one step to learn, practice at a comfortable pace, note questions for a teacher.
Never use punishment, shame or forced participation.

### ACT-006 Arabic Sound Discovery
Age: 4+, adaptable. Time: 5 min.
Steps: Listen to licensed, verified pronunciation audio; match a sound to its written letter; repeat optionally. Avoid automated correctness claims without validated speech technology.

### ACT-007 Ramadan Family Planning
Age: all. Time: 15 min.
Steps: Discuss what Ramadan means, choose a shared meal or charitable activity, plan age-appropriate participation, and identify health considerations.
No child fasting prescriptions; refer health questions appropriately.

### ACT-008 Family Story Time
Age: 3–12. Time: 10 min.
Steps: Parent reads an approved Islamic story; child describes what happened; family discusses one character choice.
All prophetic narratives require source review.

### ACT-009 Grandparent Conversation Preparation
Age: 6–18. Time: 10 min.
Steps: Parent and child list questions relatives may ask; rehearse kind explanations and respectful boundaries; avoid rehearsing deception.
Prompt: 'How can we be kind even when we disagree?'

### ACT-010 Family Charity Project
Age: all. Time: flexible.
Steps: Choose a no-cost or low-cost way to help a neighbor or community, obtain consent where necessary, reflect privately.
No donation pressure.

### ACT-011 Reflection Walk
Age: all. Time: 10–20 min.
Steps: Notice natural surroundings, ask open questions, discuss gratitude and care for creation.
Accessible alternative: window observation, indoor sensory activity or photo exploration.

### ACT-012 Source Detective
Age: 13–18. Time: 15 min.
Steps: Present an approved verse, translation, scholarly explanation and practical suggestion as separate cards. Identify which is which.
Never present a fabricated hadith or disputed claim as settled.

### ACT-013 Family Dua Notebook
Age: 6+, with caregiver. Time: 10 min.
Steps: Record personal hopes and questions. Learn reviewed duas with source attribution. Protect private entries.

### ACT-014 Ramadan Kindness Calendar
Age: 3–12. Time: 5 min/day.
Steps: Pick a simple kindness activity; mark participation if desired; allow missed days without penalty.

### ACT-015 Teen Conversation Walk
Age: 13–18. Time: 20 min.
Steps: Parent asks what their teen wants to discuss, listens without interrupting, acknowledges uncertainty, and agrees on a follow-up if appropriate.
No surveillance of a teen's private beliefs.

### ACT-016 Family Learning Review
Age: all. Time: 10 min.
Steps: Each person can name a favorite activity and one change for next week. Parent adjusts the plan.
No score of religious worth.
