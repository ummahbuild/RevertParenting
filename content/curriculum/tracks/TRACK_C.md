# Track C — Early Childhood (10)

**Status:** draft lesson design. Source references are *candidate research leads*, not verified lesson evidence. Qualified scholarly and child-safety review required before publishing.

## C01: Loving Care

**Parent learning objective:** Explain loving care in plain language, identify what is known and what needs scholarly clarification, and prepare to discuss it respectfully with a child.

**Parent primer:** Start with the parent's current understanding. Clarify the distinction between Islamic teaching, interpretation, family tradition and a practical parenting choice. Invite honest questions rather than pretending to know everything.

**Research leads:** Muslim 2319a. Verify actual relevance, context, exact wording, translation rights and any hadith grading before associating these sources with the lesson.

**Age-specific scripts and activities:**
- **0–2:** Caregiver models a gentle related habit during daily care; no child-directed screens.
- **3–5:** 'What do you notice about loving care?' Use a simple drawing, object or short story.
- **6–8:** 'Let's explore one thing about loving care together.' Use a matching or drawing activity.
- **9–12:** 'What questions would you ask about loving care?' Explore one everyday example and a reliable source.
- **13–15:** 'How does this topic connect to your experiences?' Listen to concerns and respect independent perspectives.
- **16–18:** 'What does a primary source establish, and what do scholars interpret?' Compare evidence and explanation.

**Family practice:** Set aside five minutes. Each person shares a question about loving care; choose one question to explore using an approved source or qualified teacher. For a screen-free option, use a question card and family discussion.

**Parent reflection:** What did I learn? What did my child ask? What needs further checking? No scoring of religious worth.

**Accessible formats:** plain text, audio transcript, printable conversation card, large type, RTL-ready layout, low-stimulation option.

**Acceptance criteria:** age-adapted explanation, clear source provenance, safe family interaction, review of differing opinions where relevant, rights-cleared assets, scholar and safeguarding approval.

**Publication status:** DRAFT / NOT VERIFIED.
