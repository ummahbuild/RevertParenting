# Track J — Family Seasons

Draft learning designs, pending religious and safeguarding review.

## J01 — First Ramadan

**Parent primer:** Discuss first ramadan with sensitivity to the family's circumstances. Distinguish established Islamic teachings from personal traditions and practical parenting choices.

**Source research:** Qur'an 2:183 and 17:23 are broad thematic candidates only; reviewers must verify relevance and replace them as needed. No claim of verification.

**Child adaptations:** Ages 0–2: model care without screens. Ages 3–5: short story and drawing. Ages 6–8: explain a concrete example. Ages 9–12: ask questions about family life. Ages 13–15: discuss choices and feelings. Ages 16–18: compare evidence, interpretation and practical options.

**Family activity:** Share one question, one feeling and one practical way to support each other this season. Offer a printable offline version.

**Review:** check sources, cultural assumptions, accessibility, child safety, scholarly differences and family-law concerns where relevant. Status: DRAFT.
