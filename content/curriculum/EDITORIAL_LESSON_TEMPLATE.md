# Editorial Lesson Template

## Identity
Lesson ID; track; title; objective; prerequisite; age bands; minutes; languages; author; version; status.

## Parent primer (complete prose required)
What is the topic? Why might this matter to a family? What does the primary evidence establish? What is interpretation or practical advice? What is uncertain?

## Source dossier
Arabic source; exact reference; named translation; license; hadith grade and grading authority where applicable; tafsir/sharh; disagreements; approved claim boundaries.

## Child variants
0–2 caregiver modeling; 3–5 concrete play; 6–8 story and demonstration; 9–12 examples and questions; 13–15 respectful dialogue; 16–18 source literacy and independent inquiry. Each needs actual child-facing words, not merely a label.

## Family experience
Opening question, step-by-step offline activity, materials, accessible alternatives, closing reflection, optional next step.

## Parent coaching
What if the child disagrees? What if the parent does not know? What if another guardian holds different beliefs? When to refer to a qualified teacher?

## Production assets
Illustration brief, voice script, printable card, captions, RTL QA, permissions and rights.

## Review
Evidence verification, scholar approval, child-development approval, safeguarding approval, translator approval, publication date, re-review date, corrections.
